Learning

Interactive notes and essays I [told Claude to] build, mostly for engineering students. Each series has its own format.

Reading Your Fits

Six short modules on checking a line fit against lab data: what the leftovers tell you, what they can't, and what to do about it. No statistics background needed; some Excel or MATLAB helps. Each one runs in your browser on simulated data, so you can push the sliders, break things and see what happens.

    Every module comes with

    An interactive page

    Sliders and simulated experiments. Nothing to install.

    Slides

    A short deck for presenting it or reviewing it quickly.

    A Python script

    Reproduces every plot. Runs as-is in Google Colab.

    Reflections

    Notes on the 11 industrial engineering and management books Professor Donnell recommends, written as I read them and as questions come up. Some are short essays; others work through the math with interactive charts. In English only.
