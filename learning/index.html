Learning

Interactive notes I [told Claude to] build for engineering students. Each one runs in your browser on simulated data, so you can push the sliders, break things and see what happens.

Reading Your Fits

Six short modules on checking a line fit against lab data: what the leftovers tell you, what they can't, and what to do about it. No statistics background needed; some Excel or MATLAB helps.

    Every module comes with

    An interactive page

    Sliders and simulated experiments. Nothing to install.

    Slides

    A short deck for presenting it or reviewing it quickly.

    A Python script

    Reproduces every plot. Runs as-is in Google Colab.
